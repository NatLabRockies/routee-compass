### How to Contribute

We welcome contributions! To ensure your time is well spent and aligns with the project's goals, please follow this process:

  1. Discuss First: Before starting work, comment on the issue to introduce yourself and briefly explain your proposed approach.
  2. Wait for Assignment: Please wait for a maintainer to officially assign the issue to you.
  3. Open a PR: Once assigned, feel free to submit a pull request.

For additional and relevant information about developing for this repo, please review the [RouteE Compass Docs](https://natlabrockies.github.io/routee-compass/intro.html).

_Note: Unsolicited pull requests for unassigned issues, or issues where no prior discussion has taken place, may be closed without review to prevent misaligned or duplicated effort._
